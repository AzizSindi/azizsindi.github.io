Ch.01 — Profile

Engineer. Builder.

I assess the structural integrity of ageing offshore structures, and I build the digital systems that monitor, model, and maintain them.

I hold a PhD in Offshore Structural Engineering and Digitalisation from University College London, where I am a Research and Teaching Assistant. My work combines coupled CFD and nonlinear finite element analysis with model verification, benchmark comparisons, inspection-data digitisation, and automated engineering checks.

My doctorate produced a complete, working digital-twin system for ageing monopile offshore wind turbines under extreme storm conditions, the basis of Digital Healthcare Engineering (DHE). It now supports a wider research trajectory of 30+ researchers and collaborators across UCL, Lloyd's Register, and partner institutions internationally.

I document technical methods, interpret model limitations, and communicate findings clearly, through teaching, peer-reviewed publications, and international presentations. Growing up in a coastal city, around the sea and my father's boats, I have always wanted to make the infrastructure we rely on at sea safer, smarter, and more sustainable.

Ch.03 — The PhD system

Not a framework. A working system.

Doctoral thesis · UCL · 2026Digital Healthcare Engineering for Life-Cycle Safety and Sustainability of Ageing Monopile Offshore Wind Turbines under Extreme Storm Conditions

I built a high-fidelity digital-twin system that runs the full chain, from sensors on the structure to maintenance decisions on shore, and tested it in the field.

01

Sense

Ultrasonic NDT and environmental sensors digitise damage and conditions on the structure.

02

Transmit

Secure transfer of field data from offshore to shore-based analytics.

03

Model

A coupled CFD–FEA digital twin, updated with the measured damage.

04

Diagnose

AI diagnostics assess structural health and recommend action.

05

Predict

Time-variant prognostics plan inspection and maintenance across the lifecycle.

  • End to end, not in parts. Monitoring, transmission, simulation, diagnosis, and prognosis are connected, with bespoke software automating data processing, simulation, and lifecycle assessment.
  • Field-tested. Portable ultrasonic sensing and tablet-based damage digitisation on in-service structures, plus physical testing of real-time monitoring and data transmission.
  • Engineered for extremes. Limit-state assessment of corroded, cracked, and dented monopiles under combined storm wind, waves, and rotor operation.
  • Proven in the literature. Eight peer-reviewed outputs, including journals from Cambridge University Press, Wiley, and Taylor & Francis.
